# US-04 — Quote grounding assertion

As a reader, I want every quote the AI shows me to really exist in the paper, so that I can trust the map and the chat.

## Acceptance tests
- **AT-04.1** Given page words, when a quote differs only by case, whitespace, punctuation, line-break hyphenation or ligatures then it is found with its page and per-line rectangles — Levels: unit
- **AT-04.2** Given a quote that is absent, shorter than the minimum length, or contains an ellipsis, then verification fails — Levels: unit
- **AT-04.3** Given failed quotes, when grounding runs then they are sent back to the LLM for correction up to `grounding_retries` times and corrected ones are accepted — Levels: unit, integration
- **AT-04.4** Given quotes still failing after the retries, then they are dropped, counted in the job report, and never persisted — Levels: unit, integration
- **AT-04.5** Given a map generated by the real LLM, then every displayed quote highlight sits on real text of the paper — Levels: system
